# Cermin

Indonesian for "mirror". A personal tool that turns a stream of short, random thoughts into insights the thinker would otherwise lose or never notice: forgotten ideas resurfaced, related thoughts connected, recurring patterns revealed.

## Language

An _Avoid_ list governs words: prose, chat replies and test names. A code or schema identifier may use a short form of a term, such as "ack" for Acknowledgement, as long as it uses the same form everywhere.

**Thought**:
A single free-text message captured into Cermin, in English or Bahasa Indonesia. Permanent once captured: it can be deleted, which erases it completely, but never edited.
_Avoid_: Note, post, entry, tweet

**Thought time**:
When you had the Thought, taken as the moment you sent it and read in your home timezone (WIB). Not when Cermin received or processed it.
_Avoid_: Created at, timestamp, received time

**Capture**:
The act of sending a Thought into Cermin.
_Avoid_: Log, post, tweet, submit

**Acknowledgement**:
Cermin's reply to a Capture, showing the Thought's Kind and Topics. It describes a single Thought, so it is not an Insight and takes no Reaction.
_Avoid_: Receipt, confirmation, ack

**Ask**:
A question you put to Cermin about your Corpus, marked with an explicit command. Anything sent without the command is a Thought, even if it is phrased as a question.
_Avoid_: Query, search, prompt

**Corpus**:
The full collection of captured Thoughts; the raw material every Insight is drawn from.
_Avoid_: Thought corpus, journal, database

### Classification

**Kind**:
The shape of a Thought, from a small set that changes only when you decide to. It starts as idea, question, observation, reflection and plan. A "business idea" is Kind idea with Topic business.
_Avoid_: Type, category

**Topic**:
The subject of a Thought (coding, business, philosophy, ...). A Thought has 1 to 3 of them, and Cermin keeps those likely to be right. Open-ended: Cermin assigns them, and new Topics emerge as the Corpus grows. Broad areas of life (work, job hunt, health) are Topics too. Cermin's Topics are broad areas many Thoughts share; a narrow subject worth tracking is a Declared Topic.
_Avoid_: Category, tag, label, activity category, area

**Declared Topic**:
A Topic you create yourself with a name and a description, such as a project, a book or the job hunt. Cermin uses your exact name, applies it to past Thoughts too, and stops using it once you archive it.
_Avoid_: Project, custom topic, pinned topic

**Assignment**:
One Kind or Topic given to a Thought, either by Cermin with a confidence, or by you through a Correction. Kind and Topic names are English, except a Declared Topic's.
_Avoid_: Label, tag, prediction

**Classification**:
The Kind and Topics a Thought was given at one moment, by Cermin or by you in a Correction. A Thought keeps every Classification: the latest is current, and Cermin's first is what accuracy is measured against.
_Avoid_: Label, tagging, classification run

**Correction**:
Your reply that gives a Thought a new Classification, replacing its Kind or its whole set of Topics.
_Avoid_: Edit, fix, override

### Insights

**Insight**:
Anything Cermin tells you about the Corpus that no single Thought says on its own.
_Avoid_: Analysis, report, summary

**Resurfacing**:
An Insight that brings back past Thoughts related to the one just captured.
_Avoid_: Reminder, recommendation, match

**Loop**:
A Topic or question you keep coming back to across weeks. Questions are never marked open or closed; a question becomes a Loop only by recurring.
_Avoid_: Rumination, repeat, obsession, open question

**Portrait**:
A dated, descriptive picture of how you think, drawn from the whole Corpus. Every claim in it points to the Thoughts behind it.
_Avoid_: Profile, personality, persona, type

**Digest**:
A weekly summary of Insights that Cermin sends without being asked.
_Avoid_: Report, newsletter, recap

**Reaction**:
Your one-tap verdict on an Insight: 👍 useful or 👎 noise.
_Avoid_: Rating, feedback, vote
