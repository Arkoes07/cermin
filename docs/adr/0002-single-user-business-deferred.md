# Single-user product; business decision deferred behind a 4-week gate

The original idea floated the project (then called Mirror) as a subscription app. v1 is built for exactly one user, the author. Running it as a business would need user accounts, billing, compliance with Indonesia's personal-data protection law (UU PDP), and the trust of people handing over their most private thoughts. That's weeks of work with no AI engineering in it, so it serves neither the personal-tool goal nor the portfolio goal. It would also mean entering a crowded market (Rosebud, Mindsera, Reflect, Mem, ChatGPT memory) without any proof that anyone keeps using the product. The business question is reopened only if checks 1–3 in the [PRD](../product/PRD.md#success-checks-end-of-week-4-2026-11-01) pass after 4 weeks of daily use.

## Consequences

- No accounts and no separation between users. Becoming multi-user later means a substantial redesign. That cost is accepted knowingly.
